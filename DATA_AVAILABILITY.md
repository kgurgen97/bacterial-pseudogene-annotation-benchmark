# Data availability

All 523 genome assemblies are public RefSeq records. Their identifiers are in `results/corpus523.tsv`. Derived tables and statistical outputs are provided under `results/`, `benchmark/results/` and `supplementary/`.

The complete supplementary tables are `S1.tsv`, `S2.tsv`, `S3.tsv`, `S4.tsv`, `S5.tsv`, `S6.tsv`, `S7.tsv`, `S7b.tsv`, `S8.tsv` and `S9.tsv`; see `supplementary/INDEX.tsv`. Original figure source data reside in `figures/data/`.

Large source assemblies, intermediate annotation outputs and external reference databases are **not all bundled**. Their availability and the computational environment are explained in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. The authors should deposit an immutable data and software release and insert its DOI into the final submission; **no archive DOI is claimed here**.
