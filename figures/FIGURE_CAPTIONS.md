# Figure captions

All figures are generated by `figures/make_figures.py` from committed result files. Every plotted
value is written to `figures/data/*.tsv` so it can be checked without reading the figure. Vector
sources are `.svg` and `.pdf`; `.png` is for drafts only.

---

## Figure 1. Study design and what the study does and does not establish.

`figures/figure1_study_design.{svg,pdf,png}` · values: `figures/data/fig1_study_design.tsv`

523 complete bacterial RefSeq assemblies across four ecological strata were annotated under four
configurations. **Pseudofinder is a post-processor of PGAP-derived annotation, not an independent
pipeline**, indicated by the red arrow. Two analysis branches follow. Left: pseudogene counts across
all 523 genomes, and a re-test of a canonical genome-reduction association in the 363 genomes carrying
the required trait annotations, compared between configurations by a direct paired contrast. Right:
the 92 genomes possessing a congeneric partner, in which the four annotations are joined into 103 224
caller-neutral loci by 50 % reciprocal overlap and partitioned into 4 886 disputed loci and 98 338 loci
all four configurations call intact; these support the dN/dS analysis and the caller-independent lesion
evidence. The lower panels state what the design establishes and what it does not; in particular the
study does not determine which configuration is correct and makes no claim about loss of gene function.

## Figure 2. Pseudogene abundance and disagreement depend on the annotation configuration.

`figures/figure2_caller_disagreement.{svg,pdf,png}` · values: `figures/data/fig2*.tsv`

**(a)** Per-genome pseudogene fraction across 523 genomes under each configuration; boxes show median
and quartiles, points are individual genomes, log scale. **(b)** Corpus-total pseudogene calls, with
each configuration's count of genomes reporting zero pseudogenes in the axis label; totals span
19.1-fold and median per-genome fractions span 77.2-fold. **(c)** Median against mean per-genome
fraction: the two diverge most for Bakta, whose 128 zero-call genomes pin its median near a floor.
**(d)** The caller-neutral locus partition, 103 224 loci at 50 % reciprocal overlap.

## Figure 3. No configuration reproduces the canonical genome-reduction association.

`figures/figure3_downstream_consequence.{svg,pdf,png}` · values: `figures/data/fig3*.tsv`

**(a)** Spearman ρ for every caller × metric × stratification re-test of the association between
pseudogene burden and genome size, 363 genomes. The literature predicts a **negative** correlation;
points right of zero are opposite to the prediction. Colour denotes configuration, ringed points are
significant at p < 0.05. **(b)** Verdict counts: 30 of 48 tests are significant in the opposite
direction and 18 are non-significant; none reproduces the predicted direction. Because all four
configurations agree in direction, this is a result about the claim rather than about the pipelines;
the direct between-configuration comparison is given in the text and in
`DIRECT_CALLER_EFFECT_COMPARISON.md`.

## Figure 4. dN/dS at disputed loci, its mechanical component, and its sensitivity to analytical choice.

`figures/figure4_dnds_sensitivity.{svg,pdf,png}` · values: `figures/data/fig4*.tsv`

**(a)** Exact same-gene control: median change in ω when a known lesion is introduced into a gene and
compared against the unmodified gene through the identical pipeline. Untouched genes give exactly zero
in all 225 cases; a single frameshift raises ω by **+0.0315**, the size of the whole reported effect,
while a premature stop leaving the frame intact raises it by +0.0001. **(b)** Effect of the orthologue
reference: replacing Pseudofinder's filtered reference with each partner's own RefSeq annotation across
92 genomes. **(c)** The ω gap across sensitivity arms with genus-clustered 95 % intervals: the full
locus universe at genus level, and the 36 535-locus subset unfiltered, restricted to
reciprocal-best-hit-confirmed loci, and additionally requiring synteny. Error bars are genus-clustered
bootstrap intervals; point estimates are medians of per-genome gaps.

## Figure 5. Caller-independent lesion evidence on extended intervals.

`figures/figure5_lesion_evidence.{svg,pdf,png}` · values: `figures/data/fig5*.tsv`

Lesion classification from DIAMOND alignment geometry against an orthologue, with each locus interval
extended by max(300 bp, half the orthologue length); no caller output is consulted during
classification. **(a)** Class composition for disputed loci and for loci all four configurations call
intact. **(b)** Per-genome lesion rate for the two locus classes, one line per genome, 12 of 12 genomes
higher at disputed loci. **(c)** Effect of extending the interval, against the earlier
annotation-bounded analysis: frameshift recall rises from 4.4 % to 65.9 % while internal-stop recall
falls from 82.4 % to 65.9 %, a trade-off rather than a tuning artefact. **(d)** Controls. The
synthetic arm is an **artificial-lesion detection control** — it establishes that a frameshift the
classifier is known to contain is detected (96.9 %), not real-world biological sensitivity. The
4 489-locus arm is an **annotation-consensus control**, not independently verified true negatives.
